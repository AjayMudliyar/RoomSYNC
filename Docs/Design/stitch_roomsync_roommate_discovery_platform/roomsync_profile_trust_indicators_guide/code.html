What happens if an employee leaves their verified company? expand_more

RoomSync executes silent cryptographic OAuth2 refreshes every 90 days. If an enterprise email is deactivated, bounced, or fails SAML renewal, the Workplace SSO badge automatically uncouples within 48 hours without penalizing their Aadhaar standing.

Is my 12-digit Aadhaar number stored on RoomSync servers? expand_more

Never. RoomSync uses MeitY’s secure DigiLocker OAuth protocol. We only receive an encrypted hash confirming identity validity, first/last name, and date of birth. Your 12-digit Aadhaar number, biometric data, and personal address never touch our database.

How do we resolve a dispute if someone breaks the Living Covenant? expand_more

The Living Covenant acts as a mutually signed charter. In case of repeated quiet-hour or bill-splitting disputes, roommates can initiate RoomSync Mediation. Persistent verified violations will temporarily lock the member’s Covenant badge and impact their Compatibility Score.

Can freelancers or students without corporate SSO get verified? expand_more

Yes. Students can verify through official .edu / .ac.in university domains. Freelancers and independent creators complete Tier 1 Aadhaar KYC and can optionally verify active GSTIN credentials or professional LinkedIn/GitHub identities.
