## Brand & Style
The design system targets young urban professionals and relocating students navigating the vulnerable and high-stakes process of roommate matching across Tier-1 and Tier-2 Indian tech hubs. The interface merges consumer fintech rigor with modern social warmth: institutional reliability sits alongside human energy.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Clean Glass & Tactile Elevation**:
- Structural grounding via ultra-dense contrast hierarchies, evoking high-security platforms.
- Warm, human micro-surfaces using precise ambient tinting, preventing sterile corporate patterns.
- Clear trust signals: multi-tier verification badges (Govt ID, Workplace/SSO, College Alumni) seamlessly integrated into everyday card architecture.
- Tactile, crisp interactions that signal decisive matchmaking (e.g., dynamic compatibility rings, modular habit matrices).

## Colors
The palette leverages a structured four-tier color system designed for high visual trust and expressive affinity.

- **Primary (`#1E1B4B` - Midnight Indigo)**: Anchors structural navigation, primary actions, critical headers, and brand weight. Communicates institutional authority, digital safety, and privacy.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` - Vibrant Emerald Teal)**: The functional heartbeat of the system. Governs compatibility indexes, harmony scoring, affirmative actions, verified status indicators, and match highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Warm Amber)**: Reserved strictly for conversion spikes, match urgency, "super-interest" pings, roommate readiness milestones, and VIP verified tiering.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate)**: High-contrast text, icons, and precise low-tier interactive markers.

### Canvas & Surface Structure
- **App Canvas**: `#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9` providing a cool, glare-free, tinted substrate.
- **Card Surfaces**: `#FFFFFF` with strict boundary containment to guarantee depth against the background.
- **Borders & Separators**: `#E2E8F0` for general borders, `#CBD5E1` for focused card shells and structural dividers.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across display, body, and label roles to maintain geometric cohesion and geometric warmth.

- **Headlines & Scores**: Heavy weights (`700`, `800`) feature negative tracking to establish high authority. The dedicated `metric-display` role handles compatibility numbers (e.g., `96% Match`) with tabular lining figures.
- **Body Content**: Set at `400` weight with spacious line heights to maximize scan speed across dense roommate profiles, habit preference cards, and commute matrices.
- **Micro-Copy & Tags**: Set with slight uppercase or tight uppercase tracking for verification statuses, house rules, and habit tags.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout operates on an 8pt dynamic scale within a responsive fluid grid.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column grid, max canvas width 1280px, 32px gutters (`gutter-desktop`), 48px outer margins (`margin-desktop`). Split-pane layout prioritizes fixed discovery filters on the left with a fluid 2-column or 3-column candidate profile feed on the right.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, 24px gutters, 24px margins. Filters collapse into an off-canvas drawer; card listings reflow to a 2-column pattern.
- **Mobile (<= 767px)**: 4-column grid, 16px gutters (`gutter-mobile`), 16px margins (`margin-mobile`). Single-column card stacks with sticky bottom navigation and contextual swipeable action sheets.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on deliberate multi-plane card stacking, high-blur ambient shadows, and crisp inner rim borders.

- **Flat/Ground Tier (Base Surface)**: `#F8FAFC` slate canvas with zero elevation.
- **Resting Cards**: `#FFFFFF` background with an inset hairline border (`border: 1px solid #E2E8F0`) paired with an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Raised Interactive Cards (Hover/Selected)**: Micro-lift of -2px with dual shadows: `0 12px 30px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08)` and subtle emerald border highlight (`#0D9488`).
- **Floating Overlays & Modals**: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.18)`, overlaid on top of a 12px backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).
- **Match Compatibility Rings**: Rendered using inner-glow circular rings with secondary gradient fills (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0D9488, #10B981)`) floating over a card's top-right perimeter.

## Shapes
A unified rounded shape language establishes tactile, soft-edged interaction surfaces without degrading structural alignment.

- **Primary Action Buttons, Inputs, & Tags**: Rounded at `0.75rem` (12px) to match touch targets.
- **Profile Cards & Modal Containers**: Built with `rounded-lg` (16px) or `rounded-xl` (24px) corners, framing large user imagery, lifestyle badges, and modular habit tabs.
- **Status Badges & Compatibility Indicators**: Full pill treatment (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for verification markers, interest pills, and numerical scores.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: `#1E1B4B` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `title-sm` typography, 12px border radius. Hover transitions to `#0F172A` with a soft lift.
- **Match CTA**: High-affinity actions (e.g. "Connect", "Message") use `#0D9488` background, `#FFFFFF` text, and a glowing focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: White background, `#1E1B4B` text, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border. Hover introduces `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Lifestyle Tags
- **Habit & Preference Chips**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F172A` text, 8px vertical / 14px horizontal padding.
- **Selected Filter State**: Soft emerald background (`#CCFBF1`), emerald border (`#0D9488`), and deep teal text (`#115E59`).
- **Verification Badges**: Micro-pill styling with an icon prefix. 
  - *Govt ID / Aadhaar Verified*: Soft teal fill with `#0D9488` badge text.
  - *Company / Alumni Verified*: Soft slate fill (`#E2E8F0`) with deep midnight text.

### Cards
- **Discovery Profile Card**: White base, 20px radius, 1px subtle neutral border (`#E2E8F0`). Divided into 3 horizontal zones: 
  1. Top: Hero photo with integrated compatibility percentage badge floating on top right.
  2. Center: Name, age, occupation/firm, verified micro-badges, and rent budget.
  3. Bottom: Key lifestyle chips (e.g. "Early Bird", "Vegetarian", "Non-Smoker", "Hybrid Work") and immediate action CTAs.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular checks for single-choice roommate preferences, rounded square (6px radius) for amenities. 
- In active states: Filled with `#0D9488` containing an optical white check icon, framed with a 2px `#0D9488` border.

### Input Fields
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Radius: `12px`.
- Padding: 12px 16px. Typography: `body-md`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#0D9488` with an outer ring of `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`.

### Compatibility Meter (Specialized Component)
- Radial or pill metric with a dynamic percentage scale. Background track in `#E2E8F0`, progress bar in `#0D9488` graduating to `#10B981`. Adjacent text surfaces breakdown categories (e.g., "Sleep Schedule: 98%", "Food Preferences: 90%").
